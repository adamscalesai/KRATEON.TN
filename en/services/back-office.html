// API INTEGRATIONS

Connected to your tools,
not stuck in a corner.

A back office that doesn't talk to your other tools quickly becomes just another dashboard to check in duplicate. We connect your website, WhatsApp, your CRM and your billing into a single flow, with an admin built for your type of agency.

Website connection

Requests captured on the website (form, booking, contact request) land directly in the back office, no manual re-entry.

WhatsApp Business API

Conversations and bookings taken by the WhatsApp agent flow automatically into the same management space as the rest of the activity.

Existing CRM

When your CRM exposes an accessible API, we connect it to the back office: no more double entry between the sales tool and the operational tool.

Billing

Billing data flows between the booking, the back office and your accounting tool, without re-entering the same information three times.

The back-end admin is built for your real activity, whether that's a travel agency, a real estate agency or another type of networked business. See also the Agencies solution and the Companies solution for larger-scale integrations.
