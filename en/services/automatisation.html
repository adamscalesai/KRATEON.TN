// CLIENT REACTIVATION CAMPAIGNS

Your silent customers
are worth more than a new lead.

The system automatically spots customers who haven't bought or booked anything in your database for several months, and sends them a personalized message to reopen the conversation: a relevant offer, a reminder of what they already ordered, or simply a question about their next need. Every reply is tracked and flows back to your team, every silence triggers a spaced-out follow-up instead of a single send. It's the same follow-up engine used for payments and appointments, pointed this time at the customer base that's gone quiet.
